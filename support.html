DiscPlay support

DiscPlay helps you explore your Discogs collection, track listening history, and manage record cleaning.

For help, email support@theheibers.com.

What to include

Please include your DiscPlay version and build number, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Tell us whether you are using your Discogs collection or the Beta sample collection. Screenshots are helpful; remove any personal information you do not want to share.

Never send passwords, authorization tokens, recovery codes, or signing keys. You do not need to send your full collection or listening notes for an initial support request.

Collection and search

Collection search uses the records already stored in DiscPlay and works offline. Searching the Discogs Catalog and refreshing your Discogs collection require an internet connection. A catalog result does not automatically add a record to your collection.

Automatic refresh checks when you open or return to DiscPlay. Daily means at least 24 hours since the last successful refresh; Weekly means seven days. It does not schedule a background refresh.

Listening history

Play Now records a listen immediately. To change its date or notes, edit the existing entry in Play History. Historical Play Counts lets you record earlier listens without inventing dates.

Settings → Data & Export provides listening-history and collection-count CSV exports. These are exports, not complete backups of every app setting and field.

iCloud synchronization

Connected collection and listening data use your private iCloud database. DiscPlay Beta uses a separate container from the original app; Beta sample data stays on the device.

If synchronization fails, open the iCloud Sync diagnostics screen and copy the diagnostic report for support. Review it before sending. A successful account check alone does not mean every record has synchronized.

Keep your existing app and data while a sync problem is investigated. Contact support before reinstalling or resetting data you need to retain.

Beta feedback

You can also submit feedback through TestFlight. Include the build number and whether the issue occurs on iPhone, iPad, or both.
