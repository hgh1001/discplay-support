Privacy policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026.

DiscPlay is developed by Harlan Heiber. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@theheibers.com.

Information DiscPlay uses

DiscPlay uses your Discogs account identity, collection records, folders, and release metadata to display and refresh your collection. It stores the listening entries, dates, notes, historical play counts, cleaning information, and recommendation preferences you choose to enter. It also stores app preferences on your device.

You do not create a separate DiscPlay account. DiscPlay does not ask for or store your Discogs password. You authorize access through Discogs.

Where app data is stored

Collection and listening data are stored locally. In connected mode, DiscPlay also uses Apple's CloudKit service to synchronize this data through your private iCloud database. App operation and synchronization depend on your Apple account and device settings. The developer does not have a support interface for browsing your private collection or listening history.

DiscPlay Beta has separate cloud and credential identities from the original DiscPlay app. Its sample collection is stored locally and is not synchronized to iCloud.

Discogs authorization and the signing service

The app stores your Discogs authorization token and token secret in the device Keychain. For authorized API requests, it sends the required signing inputs over HTTPS to a DiscPlay service hosted on Cloudflare. These inputs include the request method and URL, authorization token and token secret when required, and temporary callback or verification information during authorization.

Request URLs can contain your Discogs username, release identifiers, or catalog search terms. The service returns an authorization header; the app then sends its request directly to Discogs. DiscPlay does not send your Discogs password to the signing service.

The current signing-service implementation processes these inputs to sign requests and does not write them to an application database. The service uses your IP address for short-window abuse rate limiting. Hosting and security services may also process operational information such as IP addresses, request timing, and errors. Cloudflare handles provider operational information under its privacy policy; its retention practices are separate from the support-email period below.

Support and diagnostics

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Diagnostic reports can include app/build information, account availability, record counts, sync events, and errors. Review reports and screenshots before sending; include only information needed to investigate your request.

Support information is used to respond to your request and investigate app problems. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. Our support retention policy is to delete correspondence within 12 months after the request is resolved, unless we need to retain it to meet legal obligations or resolve an ongoing dispute.

Website and service providers

DiscPlay relies on Apple for app distribution and iCloud, Discogs for collection and catalog services, and Cloudflare for request signing. The support site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes website requests under its own privacy practices. These support pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, or contact forms; email links open your chosen email application.

DiscPlay does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

Provider privacy information: Apple, Discogs, Cloudflare, and GitHub.

Your choices

You can disconnect Discogs in the app to remove its locally stored authorization credentials. Disconnecting is not the same as deleting stored collection or listening data. You can also manage the app's authorization through Discogs.

You can export listening history and collection play counts, edit or delete listening entries, and use the available history-deletion controls. Deletions in a synchronized collection can propagate to your other devices. Deleting DiscPlay history does not delete your Discogs collection. CSV exports do not contain every app field and are not a complete store backup.

To ask about removal of other stored data, contact support. Do not assume uninstalling the app deletes data already stored in iCloud or in an exported file.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when DiscPlay's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact: support@theheibers.com.
